Two sections so far: Today (the front door, three landing states) and Active Session (the heart of the app — logging, partner presence, rest). Drag artboards to reorder, click any to focus fullscreen, use the toolbar zoom to compare.

Visual system

  • Type — Geist (UI) + Geist Mono (data & labels). Tabular numerals throughout so weights/reps/times always align in columns.
  • Surface — dark, slightly cool. No gradients-for-decoration. Card elevation by 4% lightness steps, hairlines for separation.
  • Accent — a single fresh green, reserved for primary action (Start, Log, Complete, Join, Finish). Never used decoratively.
  • Partner presence — muted blue for "You" and muted amber for "Andrea". Smaller, lower-contrast than the accent so they read as ambient context, not competition.
  • Numbers are the hero — in Active Session, reps × weight hit 96px. Labels small & mono.
  • One primary action per screen — Today = Start, Active = Log Set / Finish, anchored visually at the bottom.
